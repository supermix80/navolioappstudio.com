Kulla — Support

Navolio App Studio · Version 1.0 · iOS 17+

Frequently asked questions

How do I log a feeding?
On Home, tap the channel (R, L or Btl): the timer starts. Tap again to pause, then "Save". For a bottle pick the amount in ml and the milk type. After saving you have a few seconds to tap "Undo".
How do I log a diaper change?
On the "Last change" card tap 💧 for pee (saved immediately) or 💩 / 💧💩 and choose the color. You can undo from the message that appears at the bottom.
Does the timer keep running if I close the app?
Yes. The timer state is saved and restored when you reopen the app (within 4 hours); during a feeding a Live Activity also appears on the Lock Screen.
What is the daily milk need?
Enter your baby's weight in Settings → Profile: Kulla shows an indicative value (weight in kg × 150 ml per day). It is a generic estimate, not a prescription: always follow your pediatrician's advice.
How do I share data with the other parent?
Settings → Family → Invite your partner, then send the iCloud invitation with Messages or Mail. The other parent opens and accepts it: you will see the same data. An iCloud account is required on both devices.
How do I leave the family or delete shared data?
Settings → Family: the inviter can choose "Delete family and iCloud data", the invitee "Leave family". Data already on the device stays until you use Settings → Reset data.
How do I remove ads?
Settings → Advertising → Remove ads (one-time purchase). On another device with the same Apple ID use "Restore purchases".
Partner notifications do not arrive
Check iOS Settings → Notifications → Kulla and that the other parent accepted the invitation. Notifications are generated when the app syncs: if you force-quit the app they may arrive late.
Does the app work offline?
Yes. Logging, timers, widgets and History work offline. Sharing with the other parent and the banner need a connection.
How do I delete all my data?
Settings → Reset data erases logs and profile on the device. If you created the family, it also deletes the shared data from iCloud and the other parent loses access. To erase only the data in iCloud use Settings → Family → Delete family.
