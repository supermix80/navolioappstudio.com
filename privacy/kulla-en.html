Kulla — Privacy Policy

Navolio App Studio · Michele Navolio

Version 1.0 Last updated: October 7, 2026 iOS 17+ Versione italiana

In short: your baby's care logs (feedings, diaper changes, weight, notes) stay on your device and, only if you choose to share them with the other parent, in your own iCloud with encrypted fields. We have no server and no user account, and we cannot read this data. Kulla shows one banner ad (Google AdMob) with your consent, removable with a one-time purchase. Your baby's care data is never used for advertising.

Michele Navolio — Navolio App Studio ("the developer") built Kulla, a diary for parents of newborns (feedings, diaper changes, timeline), free with one banner ad and an optional purchase to remove it. This document explains what data the app handles, where it is kept and what choices you have.

2.2 Data we do NOT handle

  • No account, email or phone number is required by the app
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone
  • Kulla does not use HealthKit and does not provide medical advice
  • No payment data (handled by Apple)
  • No third-party analytics SDK, and no usage statistics are sent to developer servers

The daily milk need shown in the app (weight in kg × 150 ml) is a generic guide, not a prescription or medical advice: always follow your pediatrician's guidance.

3. Sharing with the other parent (optional)

If you send an invitation from Settings → Family → Invite your partner, feeding and diaper logs are synced through Apple iCloud (CloudKit):

  • The shared space lives in your own iCloud (CloudKit private database), not on developer servers. Sharing is limited to the people you invite (no public link).
  • Only feedings and diaper changes are synced. The baby profile (name, date of birth, weight) is not synced.
  • Log fields are protected with CloudKit value encryption. Navolio App Studio has no access to this data.
  • The person who accepts the invitation can read, edit and delete the shared logs. Logs written by the person who accepts reside in the inviter's iCloud.
  • Delete family (inviter) removes the shared space from iCloud and the other parent loses access. Leave family (invitee) removes their access. In both cases the copies already on each device remain until you delete them (Settings → Reset data).
  • "Your partner logged…" notifications are generated on your device, only for recent events, without notes or the baby's name.
  • Sharing requires an iCloud account and is subject to Apple's terms and privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy).

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Kulla shows a banner ad provided by Google AdMob. Accordingly:

  • Where required (European Economic Area, United Kingdom) we ask for your consent with Google's consent form (UMP) and, on iOS, with Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. The requests appear after your first logged event, not before.
  • If you consent, AdMob may use device identifiers (including the advertising identifier), IP address, device information and ad interaction data to show relevant ads and measure their performance. If you decline, ads are not personalized and, where the law requires, are not shown.
  • Your baby's care data (feedings, changes, weight, notes) is never shared with Google or used for advertising.
  • You can change your choice in Settings → Advertising privacy options (where available) and in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Google processes advertising data under its own policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can remove ads with a one-time purchase (Settings → Advertising → Remove ads); people who purchased see no banner and no advertising consent prompts.

5. In-app purchases

The "Remove ads" purchase is handled entirely by Apple (StoreKit). The developer does not see or store payment data. You can restore it from Settings → Advertising → Restore purchases with the same Apple ID.

6. Notifications, widgets, Live Activity and Siri

  • Notifications are local (generated by the device). You can revoke permission in iOS Settings → Notifications → Kulla.
  • Widgets and the Live Activity (feeding timer) read data on the device.
  • Siri and Shortcuts are processed by Apple according to your settings; Kulla only receives the command (e.g. "log a diaper change").

7. Legal basis (GDPR)

  • Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): to provide the app's features.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): for personalized advertising/tracking (UMP and ATT), notifications and the sharing you turn on.

Care logs may contain information about your child's health (Art. 9 GDPR). You enter it, it stays on your device or in your iCloud under your control, and the developer does not access it. Processing you carry out purely for personal or household purposes generally falls under the household exemption (Art. 2(2)(c)).

9. Data retention

  • Data on the device stays until you delete it or remove the app (iOS deletes it with the app).
  • Data in the shared iCloud space stays until the inviter deletes the family or the data from iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Kulla.
  • The developer does not keep any of your logs.

10. Children

Kulla is intended for parents, not for children, and is not in the Kids Category. Information about the newborn is entered by the parent. The developer does not collect data from children.

11. Security

  • iOS device encryption.
  • CloudKit value encryption for shared logs; sharing only with the people you invite.
  • No proprietary server stores your data.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated; the current version is always available at this address and shows its update date. We will inform you in the app of material changes.
